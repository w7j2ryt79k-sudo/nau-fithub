    FAQ

    Common questions

    What should I bring?

    Your student card, a towel, a water bottle and indoor training shoes.

    What happens when the gym is full?

    The gym holds 50 people. When it is full, wait at the entrance. Check live capacity on the home page before you come.

    Do I need to book a class?

    Turn up on time. Groups are limited, so arrive a few minutes early to secure a place.

    I am new. Where do I start?

    Try Gym Orientation or Strength Basics. Both are made for beginners.

    Can I bring a friend?

    Only registered members can train. A friend can sign up on this site first.

    Gym rules

    Wipe equipment after use. Return weights to the racks. Keep phone calls outside the training floor. Ask staff if you are unsure how to use a machine.
